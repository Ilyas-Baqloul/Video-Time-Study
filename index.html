Video Time Study (vts-tool.com): time study, line balancing and layout from one video

Film a workstation, press one key when each step starts, and get the cycle times, T1, the output and the load of each operator. Then balance the line with a Yamazumi chart and move the stations on the plan with the walking paths of the operators (spaghetti diagram).

Frequently asked questions

Is Video Time Study (vts-tool.com) really free?

Yes. It is free to use, including at work, with no account and no advertising.

Is my video sent over the internet?

No. The video is read by your browser and never leaves your computer or phone. The study is kept in this browser, and you can save it as a file.

What do I need to start?

A video of the workstation, even filmed with a phone, and a recent browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Nothing to install.

How is T1 worked out?

By default, T1 is the lowest repeatable time of the work content (c) over the first 20 usable cycles. You can choose the median or the mean instead. The site also gives the precision at 95% and the number of cycles to film for ± 5%.

Can I balance a line and change the layout?

Yes. The Yamazumi chart shows the load of each operator and machine against the takt time, and you move the steps from one operator to another. On the layout, you place the stations on a plan (image, PDF or AutoCAD DXF) and the walking paths are measured. Each scenario keeps both, next to the current state.

What can I export?

PDF reports (the time study, and from Compare / Reports the line balancing, the layout or both), an A3 PDF presentation of the layout, an Excel workbook with the plans, and your own standard sheets filled in if you use them.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, once the page is open. The menu also gives an offline copy in a single file. Only reading PDF plans and converting iPhone videos need internet the first time.

In which languages?

English, French, German, Spanish, Czech, Italian, Polish and Portuguese, each with its own page.
