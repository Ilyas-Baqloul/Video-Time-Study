How to use Video Time Study

Video Time Study (VTS, vts-tool.com) is a free tool that runs in your browser. You film a workstation, press one key each time a step starts, and the site gives you the cycle times, T1, the line balancing (Yamazumi) and the layout with the walking paths, then PDF reports, an Excel workbook and, if you use them, your own standard sheets. Nothing to install, no account: the video stays on your computer or your phone.

1. The workflow at a glance

  1. Film the workstation: a phone held still, at least 10 full cycles.
  2. Choose the video: it opens in your browser and is never uploaded.
  3. List the steps of each operator and each machine, with their type.
  4. Record: play the video and press a key when each step starts.
  5. Read the results: cycle times, T1, the reference cycle and the output.
  6. Improve in scenarios: balance the line and move the stations on the layout, next to the current state.
  7. Share: PDF reports, an A3 presentation of the layout, an Excel workbook and your filled standard sheets.

New to the tool? Open the example first (see below): everything is already filled in, so you can see each screen before you film.

2. Before you start

What you need

A video of the workstation (a phone is enough) and a recent browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Nothing to install, no account, no advertising. The tool is free, including at work.

Your data stays with you

The video is read by your browser and never leaves your computer or phone. The study is saved automatically in this browser. To keep it, move it to another computer or send it to a colleague, use Save study file in the Study tab (see Save, reopen and share).

Try the example

On the start screen, click Try an example, then Load the example. It opens Line A: 2 operators and a press, a fictional video with its study ready: the steps listed, a dozen timed cycles, a periodic task, a takt time, a balancing scenario and its layout. You can change everything. Your own study is put aside and comes back when you click Leave the example. Look at Results first, then Line balancing and Layout.

Without internet

Once the page is open, the tool works without internet. Menu, Offline copy downloads the whole tool as one HTML file that you can open on any computer. Only two things need the internet, the first time: reading a PDF plan and converting an iPhone video (a small reader or converter is downloaded, your files are not sent).

The top of the page

  • The study name, which you can type straight in the bar.
  • Line balancing and Layout open the two other views; ← Time study or ← Back to the time study brings you back.
  • Help gives the keys and the words of the results. ? opens it too.
  • Menu: the languages (English, French, German, Spanish, Czech, Italian, Polish, Portuguese), the theme (auto, light or dark), New study, Offline copy, Contact and feedback and What’s new.

3. Film the workstation

  • Hold the phone still: on a tripod or set down, in landscape, without zooming.
  • Frame the hands and the whole workstation, with the parts and the places the operator walks to.
  • Film at least 10 full cycles, ideally a few more: the site only keeps the usable ones (see Read the results).
  • 720p at 30 frames per second is enough: lighter files, the same precision.
  • Tell the operators first: you film the workstation to make the work easier, not to judge people.
  • On a phone, Film with the camera records the video straight into the site. That video is kept in this browser only: use Save the video to keep it on the phone.

iPhone: Settings, Camera, Formats, Most Compatible, and Record Video, HDR Video off. The video then opens everywhere. Otherwise the site converts it for you (see Troubleshooting).

4. Start a study

  1. Click Choose video file in the middle of the video area, or drop the file there. Browsers read MP4 (H.264), WebM and most MOV files.
  2. Open the Study tab and fill in what you know:
    • Study name: it names your files (for example: Line 4, current state).
    • Reference: the part or the product. It goes into the standard sheets.
    • Plant, Line or machine, Observer, Date.
    • Customer takt time (s): needed for the takt line, the load of each operator and the line balancing. Calculate works it out from the Available time (min) and the Customer demand (parts) over the same period (one shift, one day…): the available time divided by the demand. Available time is the opening time minus the breaks.
    • Frame step: automatic (the frame rate of the video is detected) or a fixed value.
    • How T1 is taken: the method and the rounding (see Read the results).

New study, in the menu, clears the current study from this browser. You can keep the same video or take another one, and keep the operators and the steps (the times are cleared). Save a study file first if you want to keep the current one.

5. List the steps

Everything is in the Steps tab. Do it before you record: the keys come from this list.

Timelines: one per operator and per machine

Each operator and each machine has its own timeline on the same video, with its own list of steps. Add, next to Timeline, gives Add operator and Add machine. Delete this timeline removes it with its steps and its times.

Quick start

Quick start gets everything ready in one go: give the number of Operators, Machines and Steps per timeline, tick Number the steps 10, 20, 30 (A10, B10… with several timelines), then Get ready. The steps are named Step 1, Step 2…: give them their real names before you record.

Each step

ColumnWhat it is
KeyThe key that records it: 1 to 9, 0, then letters. It is shown on each step.
Code10, 20, 30… Codes 10, 20, 30 numbers the whole list.
NameWhat the operator does, in a few words.
TypeOperation, Inspection, Walk, Wait or Machine, the types of standardized work. The colours of the whole tool follow them.
Station / Dist. (m)The station where the step is done, named as on the layout (the names already typed are offered). For a walk, its distance in metres instead, if you know it: the layout measures the walks too.
  • Write the steps in the order of the cycle.
  • Put every walk and every wait in the list, where it happens in the cycle: the line balancing, the layout and the standard sheets need them.
  • Cycle starts with: the step that opens a new cycle (by default, the first one).
  • Renaming, retyping or reordering a step (Move up, Move down) keeps its recorded times.

Paste a list from Excel

Paste list: one step per line, Code; Name; Type; Station, with the distance in metres in place of the station for a walk. Columns copied from Excel work too, also with five columns (Code, Name, Type, Distance, Station). The type and the station are optional; French words work too. For example:

CodeNameTypeStation or distance
M10Load part in thermoformerOperationThermoformer
M20Walk to waterjetWalk4
Q30Check trim edgesInspectionWaterjet
M40Wait for waterjetWaitWaterjet

Replace list keeps the recording of the steps whose code stays the same; Add to list adds the new steps at the end. Copy list copies yours, to paste it in Excel or in another study.

Parts per cycle

For a tool with several cavities, set Parts per cycle: the output and the line balancing then use the time per part.

Periodic tasks

Some tasks do not come back every cycle: a stillage change, the waste bin, a material replenishment. During the recording, P notes the ones you see. Under the list, Periodic tasks shows them and + Periodic task not filmed adds the others with their time. For each one, set How often: every how many parts it comes back. Its time is then spread over those parts in the cycle, the line balancing and the sheets. A task without a frequency does not count yet.

6. Record the video

Open the Record tab.

  1. Play the video with Space. Record at speed 1 or slower: at speed 2, your reaction time counts double.
  2. When a step starts, press its key, or Enter for the next step of the list. A step ends when the next one starts.
  3. On a touch screen, tap Record next step or the button of the step.
  4. Go on cycle after cycle. At the very end, press X: End of study ends the last step.
  • Under the video, the band shows the recorded steps in their colours. Click it to go to that time, drag it to move along the video, and zoom on 20 s, 1 min, 2 min or 5 min. The bar below it shows the whole video.
  • Cycle, Current step and Next planned tell you where you are.
  • To move in the video: ← → 1 s back or forward (Shift for 5 s), , . one frame, [ ] slower or faster.
  • The keys work when the page has the focus. If Keys off: click on the page shows, click on the page. They are off while you type in a field.
  • If you record earlier in the video, where steps are already recorded after that time, the site asks before replacing them.

Mark what goes wrong

Use these keys, or More events (issue, interruption, periodic task…) under the video. A cycle with an issue, an interruption or something out of view stays visible but is not used for T1.

KeyEventWhen and what it does
FIssueThe current step takes longer because of a problem (defect, breakdown, missing material…). The time stays in the step; the cycle is not used for T1 and the reference cycle.
IInterruptionThe operator stops working. Choose the cause; when the work starts again, press the key of the step.
PPeriodic taskStillage change, waste bin, replenishment. Its time stays out of (a) and the cycle stays usable; with its frequency, it is spread over the parts.
VOut of viewThe operator is hidden or the video is cut.
WWalkA walk that is not in the list.
AWaitA wait that is not in the list.
XEnd of studyEnds the last step.

Walks and waits noted with W or A can become real steps: Put them in the list, in the Steps tab, places each one right after the step it follows (for example: Walk after 011). The times do not change. Rename them and add the distances.

Fix a mistake

  • Wrong key: Ctrl+Z right away (Ctrl+Y redoes).
  • Later: Fix a step, in the Record tab, lists the step in progress and the 4 before it, with Change and Delete.
  • Click the current step under the video, or press and hold a step on the band (right-click on a computer): change the step, move its start by 0.1 s, start it at the video position, or delete it.
  • Delete deletes the step in progress (press it twice).
  • In the Log tab, every event can be moved, changed or noted (see below).

7. Read the results

Open the Results tab. Each timeline has its own results.

The cycles

Each cycle shows its start, its cycle time (a) without the periodic tasks, its wait (b) and its work content (c) = (a) − (b), and whether it is used. A cycle is usable when it is complete, with no issue, no interruption and nothing out of view. 10 usable cycles are needed, and at most the first 20 are used, as on a time measurement sheet.

T1 and the reference cycle

  • T1, by default, is the lowest repeatable time of standardized work: the smallest work content (c) is left out (it may be a mistake), then T1 is the smallest time that comes back at least twice. It is the time a trained operator holds at a normal pace.
  • You can take the median or the mean of the cycles instead: Change the method, or How T1 is taken in the Study tab. The rounding is to the tenth of a second, to the second, or automatic (the tenth under 20 s). These are observed times: no pace rating and no allowances are added.
  • The reference cycle is the real usable cycle closest to T1. Its step times go to the line balancing, the layout and the sheets.

The other figures

  • Precision (95 %): how precise T1 is with the cycles you have, and how many cycles you need for ± 5 %. The spread and the standard deviation of (c) show how steady the work is.
  • Output: parts per hour of the reference cycle with the periodic tasks (per part when there are several parts per cycle).
  • Steps: the time of each step over the cycles used, and in the reference cycle.
  • Periodic tasks: their time, how often they come back, and their share per part.
  • Operator load: the time per part of each operator, by step type, against the takt line (enter the takt time in the Study tab).

Your results

PDF report gives the time study report, Excel workbook the whole study in one workbook, and Other exports… the summary and the log as CSV files or ready to paste in Excel. The separators are set in the Study tab: comma and point for Excel in English, semicolon and comma for Excel in French.

The log

The Log tab lists every event in order, cycle by cycle, with the reason when a cycle is not used. For each event: Go to this time, Change step, Note, the cause of an interruption or the issue, 0.1 s earlier or 0.1 s later, and Set to video time. Show current event finds the event at the position of the video.

8. Balance the line (Yamazumi)

Click Line balancing at the top of the page.

Reading the chart

  • One column per operator, plus the machines. Each block is a step, as high as its time in the reference cycle, in the colour of its type. Hover a block, or tap it, to read it in full.
  • The red dashed line is the customer takt time. The solid blue line is the line cycle, set by the bottleneck. The part of a column above the takt is marked in red.
  • Above each column: its cycle and its gap to the takt, its load (the work without the waits, in % of the takt), and how long it waits for the bottleneck at each cycle.
  • A machine counts for its active time only: its idle time, greyed, does not count. Time the loading and the unloading as machine steps, because the machine is busy then. Machines never count as operators.

The figures

FigureHow it is worked out
BottleneckThe longest cycle, operators and machines together.
Output at the bottleneck3600 / bottleneck, in parts per hour.
Theoretical operatorsTotal work content Σ(c) / takt, rounded up.
Line balanceΣ(c) / (number of operators × bottleneck). 100 % means everyone works for the whole bottleneck cycle, with no waiting.

In a scenario, each figure shows its gap to Current, in green when it is better.

Current and scenarios

  • Current is what you timed. It follows the time study and cannot be changed here.
  • + New scenario creates a scenario. Start from: Current (as measured) or another scenario. A scenario holds both a line balancing and a layout, with its own plan. You can make up to 30.
  • When you create a scenario, the site asks what to do with the measured waits: keep them, to review (they are hatched), or put them aside. They often come from the imbalance itself and change once steps move.
  • Edit scenario renames, copies or deletes it, and keeps a note: what this scenario changes, why, what to check. The note shows in both views and in the reports.

Changing a scenario

  • Drag a block to another column, or higher or lower in the same one, with the mouse or a finger. A step given to another operator shows the ⇄ mark.
  • Click a block to change its time, name, type or column, split it in 2, delete it, or write the kaizen action behind the change (for example: box moved closer).
  • + Operator adds a column, + Step adds a step by hand, and the ⋯ button of a column renames or deletes it. Unassigned steps keeps what you take out of the columns.
  • A dotted line shows the total of the same column in Current. The line In this scenario: sums up the changes, the new line cycle and the output.
  • Esc cancels a move in progress; Undo and Redo work as everywhere.
  • Layout of this scenario → opens the same scenario on the plan.

9. Draw the layout and the walking paths

Click Layout at the top of the page. The bar of the scenarios is the same as in the line balancing: Current, your scenarios, + New scenario, Compare / Reports, and the lock of the reference.

1. The plan (optional)

  • Import a plan (picture, PDF or DXF). For a picture (PNG, JPG) or a PDF, set the scale: click both ends of a known distance on the plan, then type its real length in metres. For a PDF, choose the page; it stays sharp when you zoom in.
  • For an AutoCAD drawing (DXF, saved as ASCII): choose the units, the layers to show, and the blocks that are machines or stations. Each block becomes a station with its real outline, at scale. DWG files cannot be read: save the drawing as DXF.
  • Without a plan, one grid square is one metre.
  • Plan opacity fades the plan; Hide the plan takes it away for a moment.
  • The plan stays on your computer, in this browser, never in the study file. On another computer, load the same file again: the stations stay where they are.

2. The stations

  • Choose a type (Work station, Machine, Rack or bin, Table) and click on the plan to place it.
  • Cut out of the plan: click the corners round a machine of the plan, or drag a rectangle. The part cut out becomes a station you can move, with its real look. Draw an outline gives a station that exact outline.
  • Drag a station to move it, its corner to resize it, its round handle to turn it (15° steps, free with Shift). Click it to rename it, resize it, turn it, duplicate it or delete it.
  • Keys: the arrows move the selected station by 10 cm (50 cm with Shift), R turns it by 15° (Shift+R the other way), Delete removes it.
  • Stations named in the steps and not on the plan yet are listed under Stations named in the steps: click a name, then the place where it stands on the plan (the next one follows), or Put them all in a row and drag them to their place.

3. Where each step is done

  • Choose an operator and answer the questions, step by step: Where does the cycle start?, Where is this step done?, Where does this walk lead?
  • To answer, click one of the round spots around a station: that is where the operator stands (front, back, left or right of it).
  • When the step has a station in Steps, the question goes straight to it: only its round spots are shown, and you click the one where the operator arrives. Another place than a station shows all the spots again. A step at another station than the step before it, with no walk between, gets its own question.
  • The steps between two walks are done at the same place. To place one elsewhere, click it in the round.
  • To go round a machine or follow an aisle, click an empty spot of the plan: the walk passes there.
  • On the plan, the arrows show the way walked, a dashed leg is walked without a part, and the numbers are the steps of the list.

Lock the reference

When the current layout is drawn, Validate the current layout → (or Lock the reference in the bar) makes it the reference: it is locked and every scenario starts from a copy of it. Unlock the reference lets you change it again; the scenarios keep their own plan.

The figures

  • For each operator: Distance per cycle, Footsteps per cycle and Walking per cycle; for the whole line, the walking over 1,000 parts.
  • Distances are straight lines between the places where the operator stands (0.40 m from the edge of a station), through the waypoints. One footstep is 0.70 m by default (Settings).
  • Walking time = distance / walking speed. The speed comes from the walks timed in the study, otherwise 1 m/s.

Improve it in a scenario

  • Open or create a scenario at the top, then Move the stations: drag and turn them, and the figures before → after follow every move.
  • What changes lists the stations and the steps that change; Detail by operator gives the distance, the footsteps, the walking time and the cycle.
  • Before / After shows the current layout in place of the scenario.
  • Play shows each operator walking their path at their speed and staying at each place for the time of their steps; when you compare, today’s path is walked too, in grey.
  • Start again from the current plan puts every station back.

Line balancing and layout are linked: a scenario is both, who does which step and where the stations stand. Move a step to another operator in the line balancing and their path changes on the plan. Move a station on the plan and the walks of the line balancing take the new times. The line cycle and the line balance show both changes.

10. Compare scenarios and make reports

Compare / Reports

Compare / Reports is in the bar of the scenarios, in both views.

  1. The states compared: tick the states to show, and choose what they are Compared with: the current state, as measured, or a scenario. Each figure shows its gap to the reference; the best figure of each line is in green.
  2. The PDF report: Line balancing only (Yamazumi compared, bottleneck, output, load of each operator, kaizen actions), Layout only (plans compared, gains in distance, footsteps and walking time for each operator) or Full: line balancing and layout. 11. Fill your own standard sheets (optional)

If your company uses the matching templates (a time measurement sheet, a standardized work file with a combination table, a work cell balancing), the site can fill them for you, one sheet per operator, keeping the formulas and the macros.

  1. In the Results tab, under Standardized work sheets (if you use these templates), choose each template once. They stay on this computer and are never uploaded.
  2. Fill for: the current state, as measured, or a scenario (a simulation: the times of the scenario, the distances and the plans of its layout; the measure sheet is only filled for the current state).
  3. Download the filled sheets. The walking distances and the plans come from the layout.

A file with macros downloaded from the internet may be blocked by Windows: the site tells you how to unblock it before you open it.

12. Save, reopen and share

  • The study is saved automatically in this browser. A private window or a full storage cannot keep it: the site warns you, then save a file.
  • Save study file, in the Study tab, writes a small .json file: the steps, the times, the scenarios and the layout. It does not hold the video or the plan. The site reminds you after 50 new steps.
  • Open study file opens it again, here or on another computer. Then choose the same video: the site checks that it is the video of the study.
  • A video filmed or converted in the site is kept in this browser only: Save the video keeps it on your device.
  • If the tool is open in two tabs and the study changes in both, the site asks which version to keep.
  • To share with someone who does not use the tool, send the PDF reports or the Excel workbook.
  • Contact and feedback, in the menu, opens your mail app with a message ready: only what you write is sent, nothing from your study or your video.

14. Troubleshooting

The video does not play

Browsers read MP4 (H.264), WebM and most MOV files. Try Chrome or Edge on a recent computer, or Safari on a Mac. Or convert the video to MP4 with VLC (free): Media, Convert / Save, Add the video, Convert / Save, profile Video - H.264 + MP3 (MP4), choose the file name, Start.

An iPhone video (HEVC)

The site offers to convert it, on your device: the video is not sent anywhere. It takes about as long as the video, more for a long or 4K one, and the page must stay open. The converted video is 720p at 30 frames per second, without the sound. The first time, the converter (about 1 MB) is downloaded. An HDR video may look duller once converted; the times do not change. Next time, film in Most Compatible (see Film the workstation).

The keys do nothing

Click on the page (Keys off: click on the page), or leave the field you are typing in.

Too few usable cycles

Film more cycles, and check the Log tab: cycles with an issue, an interruption or something out of view are not used. Only the first 20 usable cycles count.

No takt line, empty line balancing

Enter the customer takt time in the Study tab. The line balancing needs at least one complete cycle recorded, or a scenario where you add the steps by hand.

A plan does not open

Reading a PDF needs the internet the first time; otherwise save the page as a picture (PNG or JPG). A DWG drawing must be saved as DXF in AutoCAD (Save As, DXF); a binary DXF must be saved again as ASCII DXF.

The study has gone

A study lives in one browser. Save a study file regularly and open it with Open study file.
